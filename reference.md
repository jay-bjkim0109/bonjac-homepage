## Notes from the articles

- **ZDNet Korea (2026-11-13)**: Bonjac introduced a personalized fragrance recommendation technology at the China International Import Expo in Shanghai, as one of three Korean startups joining with L'Oréal. It uses Bonjac's own fragrance database and an AI curation engine to help customers find a scent that fits their taste, online and in stores. Bonjac is commercializing it on the experience of running the Korean-French niche fragrance brand Selvatico.
- **경제인뉴스 (2026-09-28)**: Bonjac runs the premium fragrance brand Selvatico, combining French fragrance and perfumery capability with Korean sensibility, sold in department stores and at Musinsa Beauty Seongsu. It is developing "Scent ID", an AI curation service that helps consumers choose a scent based on fragrance experience data, and plans to test it in stores with L'Oréal.
